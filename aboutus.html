WHERE IT ALL STARTED


Founded in 2023, Bellezapiel by Zandra Nicole Bosque was a small online store offering hand-made skincare products crafted by Zandra, a skincare enthusiast and expert. Zandra developed her own recipes by combining natural ingredients with her years of experience and knowledge of skincare.

In the beginning, she struggled to market her products, as she had no experience in online marketing and didn’t know where to start. She tried social media but didn’t see any significant increase in sales. She then decided to attend trade shows and festivals to promote her products in person. This helped her to make connections with other skincare businesses and customers.

As time went on, Zandra’s products became popular, and she was able to expand her offerings. Zandra hired a small team to help her with packaging and shipping, which allowed her focus on product development and customer service. She also started offering subscription plans, which allowed customers to receive regular shipments of their favorite products.

In 2024, Bellezapiel by Zandra was awarded the Small Business of the Year award, which increased its visibility and reputation. This led to a significant increase in sales and a steady growth of business.

Today, Bellezapiel by Zandra has a strong online presence, sells its products worldwide, and employs a team of over 20 employees. The company continues to offer high-quality skincare products crafted with love and attention to detail and established itself as a recognized and respected player in the skincare industry.
